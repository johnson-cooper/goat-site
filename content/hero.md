# Project Goat
## A retro-styled Yu-Gi-Oh! GOAT Format campaign built around dueling, progression, packs, and CPU opponents.

Enter a compact single-player campaign inspired by the atmosphere of classic Yu-Gi-Oh! games. Build your collection, challenge themed NPC duelists, unlock new tiers, and shape a deck from a card pool focused on the 2005 GOAT Format era.

> Ancient presentation. Competitive old-school rules. A campaign designed to feel like something you could have found on a mysterious game disc in 2005.
