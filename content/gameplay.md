## The Campaign Loop

Start with a limited collection and a small set of available opponents. Every duel is part of the progression loop: learn what the opponent is playing, adapt your deck, earn rewards, and decide where to invest next.

- **Choose a duelist.** NPCs use themed decks and increasingly specialized strategies.
- **Duel under GOAT-era rules.** The focus stays on the slower, interactive style that defines the format.
- **Earn credits and rewards.** Victories feed directly back into collection growth.
- **Open packs.** New packs expand the available card pool as higher tiers unlock.
- **Refine your deck.** Build around the cards you have earned and prepare for stronger opponents.

The result is intended to sit somewhere between a simulator, a deck-building sandbox, and the progression structure of classic handheld and console Yu-Gi-Oh! games.
