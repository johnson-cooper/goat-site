## What is Project Goat?

**Project Goat** is a fan-made Yu-Gi-Oh! project centered on GOAT Format and a progression-driven single-player experience. The goal is to make old-school deck building feel like a campaign rather than a bare simulator.

The project combines a dueling client with unlockable card packs, CPU-controlled opponents, tier progression, rewards, and a growing library of real GOAT deck archetypes. Its visual direction deliberately leans into dark menus, warm gold typography, and an Egyptian-inspired presentation that matches the mythology surrounding classic Yu-Gi-Oh!.

The campaign hub is designed around three simple loops: **duel**, **earn**, and **build**. Win credits, open new packs, expand your collection, and test increasingly specialized decks as new opponents become available.
