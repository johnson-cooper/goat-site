## Project Direction

Project Goat is being developed as an iterative fan project. Systems can change as the campaign, CPU behavior, balance, presentation, and content library are refined.

### Current Direction
- Expand and polish the pool of CPU duelists and historical GOAT archetypes.
- Improve opponent decision-making and duel behavior.
- Refine campaign balance, rewards, tier pacing, and pack progression.
- Continue polishing responsive UI behavior and visual consistency.
- Expand sound, music, options, and other client presentation features.
- Keep the project focused on a cohesive GOAT-era identity rather than becoming a generic all-era simulator.

### Design Goal

The long-term target is simple: make launching Project Goat feel like opening a lost old-school Yu-Gi-Oh! campaign—immediately understandable, visually distinctive, and deep enough to keep experimenting with decks.
